---
name: Skill association browsing
description: Product semantics for finding routines and playlists that use a skill.
---

A skill's activity list includes direct routine assignments and assignments on any step, including hidden steps. It also includes playlists directly assigned to the skill or containing a routine linked to it.

**Why:** The user asked for a complete view of routines and playlists tied to each reusable skill, so step-level and indirect playlist use should remain discoverable.

**How to apply:** Distinguish direct from indirect assignments and label hidden routines or steps; keep the browsing view aligned with saved assignment semantics.
