---
name: Routine categories
description: Product decision about lightweight, user-defined category labels
---

Categories are optional labels attached to individual routines. The filter choices come from labels currently in use rather than a separate category collection. Routines created before categories remain uncategorized.

**Why:** The request was to categorize and filter routines, not to manage a separate category library. Deriving choices from routines avoids orphaned or stale category entries and keeps older saved routines valid without a migration.

**How to apply:** Preserve labels when copying and transferring routines. If category-wide rename or deletion is added later, apply it to the affected routines rather than assuming independent category records exist.

Skills are a separate managed library, not another kind of routine category. A skill can be assigned to routines, playlist definitions, and individual steps.

**Why:** The user requested reusable skills that can be managed independently and assigned across multiple kinds of routine content.

**How to apply:** Keep category labels and skill assignments distinct. Renaming a skill should preserve links through its stable ID; deleting one should remove only its assignments, not the routines or playlists.
